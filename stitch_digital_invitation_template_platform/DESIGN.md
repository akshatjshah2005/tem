## Brand & Style

This design system blends **editorial romance with high-conversion modern SaaS aesthetics**. It caters to modern couples, families, and event planners seeking bespoke, mobile-first invitations for luxury weddings, milestones, and celebrations.

### Personality & Emotional Core
- **Ceremonial & Celebratory:** Deep regal indigos paired with sunlit gold and vibrant ceremonial rose evoke festivity, prestige, and joyous commitment.
- **Effortless Modernity:** Frictionless self-service workflow with instant live previews, transparent pricing, and seamless sharing on social channels.
- **Trustworthy & Pristine:** Polished glass surfaces, generous whitespace, crisp card architectures, and tactile micro-interactions that reassure users during high-stakes personal events.

### Design Movement: Modern Romantic Glassmorphism
The aesthetic combines classical stationery sensibilities with clean SaaS mechanics:
- Frosted glass floating badges with subtle specular highlights.
- Crisp white surface cards floating above soft warm-blush and ethereal ceramic backdrops.
- Sophisticated serif typography for ceremony titles balanced by a crystal-clear, modern sans-serif body for UI actions and micro-copy.

## Colors

The palette establishes an emotional duality between regal celebratory heritage and clean contemporary SaaS readability.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#1E1B4B` - Royal Indigo):** Used for commanding headlines, high-emphasis action triggers, primary navigation anchors, and deep celebratory framing.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Champagne Gold):** Used for luxury accents, star ratings, celebratory tags, gold borders, highlighted discounts, and glowing badges.
- **Tertiary (`#E11D48` - Royal Crimson / Festive Rose):** Used for focal CTAs ("Most Chosen" tier markers, love story buttons, and celebratory badges).
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Grounding neutral for ultra-readable body text, structured dividers, and subtle outlines.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm blush tint (`#FDFBF9` blending into `#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Elevation:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) card containers with low-contrast borders (`#E2E8F0` at 60%).
- **Frosted Glass Accents:** Pure white at 70% opacity with 16px backdrop-blur and a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).

## Typography

The pairing between **Playfair Display** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** bridges classical stationery craftsmanship with modern SaaS readability.

### Type Roles & Pairing Strategy
- **Headlines & Display:** Playfair Display introduces literary charm and romantic cadence. It sets an emotional tone for hero sections, pricing headers, and category showcase titles.
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers warm, geometric precision for workflow instructions, template filters, pricing features, and form elements.
- **Kickers & Overlines (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with generous letter-spacing (`0.08em`) in gold or rose hues to cleanly classify sub-sections.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid with dedicated responsive breakpoints ensures crisp presentation from mobile screens up to wide desktops.

### Breakpoints & Layout Adapters
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.5rem` outer canvas padding. Section gaps compress to `space-2xl` (`4rem`). Mobile invitation mockups collapse into single swipeable carousels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins. Side-by-side split hero elements adapt to top-to-bottom stack.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with a max container width of `1280px` centered via automatic margins and `4rem` outer padding. Section vertical separation spans `space-3xl` (`6rem`) for an airy, editorial feel.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Maintain rhythmic card paddings: `1.5rem` on mobile, scaling to `2rem` on desktop for pricing and feature cards.
- Content groupings inside cards adhere to strict multiples of `0.5rem` (`space-sm`) and `1rem` (`space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp surface layering combined with ambient shadows and soft glassmorphism.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background gradient transitions subtly from off-white blush `#FDFBF9` to ceramic cool slate `#F1F5F9`.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Solid pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`) and soft ambient shadows (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.05)`).
3. **Level 2 (Featured / Hover State):** Elevated interactive elements (such as selected template cards and the "Most Chosen" pricing box) gain dynamic depth: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.12)`, lifted slightly via `-4px` translation.
4. **Level 3 (Floating Devices & Modals):** Device mockups and contextual tooltips receive dual-layer drop shadows (`0 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.2)` plus ambient golden glow `0 0 40px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1)`).

### Glassmorphism Overlays
Floating feature badges (e.g., "Add Photos & Videos", "Track RSVPs") use an ultra-refined glass formula:
- `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
- `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
- `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`
- `box-shadow: 0 8px 16px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`

## Shapes

The geometry strikes a balance between friendly modern software and sophisticated presentation stationery.

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to form inputs, filter chips, accordion items, and secondary action tags.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used on template preview cards, feature tiles, and pricing plan containers.
- **Extra Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Used on outer mobile device frames and hero banner surfaces.
- **Pill Geometry (`9999px`):** Reserved for category tags, workflow step numbers, rating badges, and primary action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Deep Royal Indigo (`#1E1B4B`) with crisp white text, pill-shaped (`9999px`), transitioning to an assertive navy lift on hover.
- **Celebratory CTA:** Royal Rose (`#E11D48`) pill button reserved for conversion highlights and direct instant purchases.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent surface with 1.5px border (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.15)`), dark indigo text, shifting to soft gold-tinted fill (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.05)`) on hover.

### Template Cards
- Pure white container with a 4:5 aspect ratio mock screen showcasing the invitation.
- Rounded corners (`16px`), overflow hidden, bottom metadata panel containing invitation title in Playfair Display, category pill, and dual actions ("View Demo", "Customize").

### Pricing Tiers
- Transparent 1-time purchase cards. The recommended tier ("Link + Photos & Music") is elevated with a 2px rose border, a top floating badge, and high-contrast Rose CTA.
- Clear itemized feature checklist using custom check icons in soft rose or warm gold.

### 4-Step Interactive Workflow
- Horizontal progressive track on desktop with connected dotted lines.
- Numbered circular badges (`32px` diameter) in solid indigo or royal blue, paired with rounded-card task descriptions ("Choose Template", "Customize", "Share", "Track Responses").

### FAQ Accordion
- Minimal, clean horizontal white cards with low-contrast borders.
- Expanding icon changes state smoothly from plus (`+`) to close (`✕`) with zero layout shift.
